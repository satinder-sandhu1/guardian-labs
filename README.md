# Guardian Lab

**Guardian of the AI Age.**

Guardian Lab is an independent frontier AI security, control, risk and assurance research organization focused on one central mission:

> **Keep increasingly capable intelligence under meaningful human control.**

Guardian Lab exists for a world in which AI is no longer only software that answers questions. Advanced systems can reason, remember, plan, use tools, delegate, persist, interact with infrastructure, influence other systems and act with growing autonomy.

That creates two distinct security problems:

1. **Adversaries can attack, manipulate or weaponize AI systems.**
2. **AI systems themselves can become the source of risk** through deception, emergent objectives, excessive agency, strategic autonomy, resistance to intervention, uncontrolled delegation or replication, and offensive use of connected capabilities.

Guardian Lab studies both.

## The Guardian Mandate

Guardian Lab exists to stand between uncontrolled intelligence and the people, institutions and critical systems that depend on it.

Our work is organized around a simple doctrine:

**Watch → Threat-model → Break → Chain → Contain → Prove → Detect → Intervene → Learn**

We do not treat safety, alignment, sandboxing, human oversight or model refusal as conclusions. We treat them as control claims that must survive evidence-based testing.

## Research Frontiers

Guardian Lab research spans the full AI control stack, including:

- Agentic attack surfaces and excessive agency
- Prompt and context exploitation
- MCP and toolchain security
- Model and AI supply-chain security
- Memory and persistence security
- Identity, authority and delegation
- Multi-agent failure modes
- Runtime control, containment and killability
- Advanced intelligence control
- Deceptive or strategically misaligned behavior
- Loss-of-control and intervention failure
- AI risk, assurance and control validation

## Advanced Intelligence Control

A central Guardian Lab research area is the point where the intelligence itself can no longer be assumed to be a trusted component.

This includes research into:

- deceptive behavior and hidden objectives
- strategic autonomy and self-directed action
- resistance to shutdown, override or containment
- uncontrolled delegation or replication
- capability escalation across tools and infrastructure
- persistence beyond intended scope
- offensive behavior initiated or amplified by autonomous systems
- architectures that preserve independent human authority even when model behavior cannot be trusted

The objective is not to make advanced intelligence weaker. The objective is to ensure that greater capability is matched by stronger control.

## Research Standard

Guardian Lab research should be:

- **Authorized** — performed only on systems we own or have explicit permission to assess
- **Reproducible** — environments, assumptions, versions and evidence are recorded
- **Control-focused** — every failure is translated into a concrete control objective
- **Evidence-driven** — observed behavior is separated from inference and hypothesis
- **Disclosure-aware** — sensitive operational details are withheld where publication would create disproportionate risk
- **Defender-useful** — research should improve detection, containment, architecture, intervention or assurance

## Publication Model

Research progresses through:

**Private → Disclosure Pending → Public**

Only sanitized, approved public research belongs in this repository.

## Experiment Registry

Public experiments use Guardian Lab identifiers such as:

`GL-EXP-001`

Future experiments should capture the research question, threat or loss-of-control model, environment, procedure, telemetry, observed behavior, control failure, mitigation and assurance evidence.
