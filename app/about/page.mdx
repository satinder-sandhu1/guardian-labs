# About Guardian Labs

## Guardians of the AI Age

Guardian Labs is an independent AI security research lab focused on one central problem:

**How do we keep increasingly capable and autonomous AI secure, bounded, observable, interruptible, and under meaningful human control?**

AI is moving beyond passive software. Modern systems can reason, remember, call tools, access external data, delegate work, interact with other systems, and take actions that produce real-world consequences.

That creates a new security frontier.

Guardian Labs studies how those systems fail under adversarial, unexpected, and high-autonomy conditions.

## Our mission

**Keep powerful AI under meaningful human control.**

We research failures involving autonomy, hostile instructions, permissions, memory, tool use, identity, model integrity, multi-agent interaction, oversight, containment, and intervention.

## The Guardian Principle

**Capability without control becomes risk.**

The more capable AI becomes, the more important its boundaries become.

Guardian Labs exists to test those boundaries, expose where they fail, and turn the resulting evidence into stronger security controls.

## Our role

A guardian must do more than react after failure.

A guardian must:

**Watch. Challenge. Contain. Detect. Intervene. Learn.**

## Publication model

Research progresses through:

**Private → Disclosure Pending → Public**

Only sanitized, approved research is published on Guardian Labs.
