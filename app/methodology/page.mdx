# Methodology

Guardian Labs conducts authorized, controlled, evidence-driven AI security research.

## Principles

### Authorized
Test only systems we own or are explicitly permitted to assess.

### Reproducible
Capture environment, assumptions, versions, permissions, test conditions, evidence, and outcomes.

### Evidence-driven
Separate observed behavior from hypotheses and interpretation.

### Control-focused
Ask not only whether a system can be manipulated, but whether manipulation can cross into authority, action, persistence, or loss of oversight.

### Defender-oriented
Every finding should produce useful detection, containment, mitigation, or design guidance.

### Responsible disclosure
Do not publish sensitive exploit details before affected parties have had an appropriate opportunity to remediate.
