# Research

Guardian Labs investigates failure modes that emerge as AI becomes more capable, connected and autonomous.

## Research frontiers

- [Agent Security](/research/agent-security)
- [Prompt Injection](/research/prompt-injection)
- [MCP Security](/research/mcp-security)
- [Model Security](/research/model-security)

Future areas will include memory security, AI identity and authorization, multi-agent systems, RAG security, runtime security, adversarial ML and AI containment.
